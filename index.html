Meet VII

VII TORIA is Victoria Graham, a Swedish-Jamaican DJ and producer in Dubai. She reads a room in several languages and plays it in more.

  • Afrobeats
  • Amapiano
  • House
  • R&B
  • Arabic
  • Bollywood
  • Latin
  • Disco house
  • 80s–2000s
  • Based in Dubai, UAE

    UAE resident with her own car and controller. Abu Dhabi, Oman and abroad on request.

  • Heritage Sweden × Jamaica

    A multicultural ear for multicultural rooms, from English and Arabic to French and Latin.

  • Teaches Smart Gate DJ School

    DJ coach in Dubai, helping new DJs find their own sound. Lessons can be booked below.
